Malaysian Borang EA (Form C.P.8A) & Employment Act 1955 s.61 Payslip Compliance Guide

Under Section 83(1A) of the Income Tax Act 1967, every Malaysian employer must prepare and render Borang EA (Form C.P.8A — Penyata Saraan Daripada Penggajian) to all employees on or before 28 February following the end of the assessment year. Failure to furnish Borang EA on time carries penalties of RM 200 to RM 20,000 under Section 120(1)(b).

What Parts Must Be Included in LHDN Borang C.P.8A?

  • Part A (Butiran Pekerja): Full employee name, job designation, NRIC / Passport number, LHDN Tax TIN (IG/SG), KWSP member number, and active months worked.
  • Part B (Pendapatan Penggajian): Gross salary/wages, overtime, commissions, bonuses, taxable allowances, and Benefits-in-Kind (BIK).
  • Part D & E (Potongan Berkanun): Total Monthly Tax Deductions (PCB / MTD), CP38 deductions, Zakat via salary deduction, employee share of KWSP (EPF), and employee share of PERKESO (SOCSO) + EIS (SIP).
  • Section 61 Employment Act 1955 Monthly Payslips: Employers must also issue itemized payslips showing gross wages, individual statutory deductions, employer EPF/SOCSO/EIS contributions, and net pay credited.
